For clubs · iPhone & Android

Clubhand

Volunteer shifts, events and club life in one app. Organisers generate shifts in seconds, members sign themselves up, and outside helpers join via a link or QR poster — no app needed. Free for clubs with up to 30 members, data stored in Switzerland.

For sailors · iPhone & Android

NaviLog

The offline sailing logbook: automatic GPS trip logging, a physics-based anchor alarm, one-tap man overboard and a barograph from the phone's own barometer. Free, no account needed; a one-time Pro purchase adds exports and cloud backup.

Guide App Store Google Play FAQ
For charter companies & sailing schools

NaviLog Fleet

Digital boat handover: QR sticker on the boat, mandatory photos, checklist, damage report and signature, a PDF report by e-mail and a web cockpit for the whole fleet. Ends the deposit dispute before it starts. Free for skippers.
